## Publishing checklist

- Use a clear product or lifestyle photo; avoid screenshots with prices that may change.
- Instagram: link in bio, not in the caption.
- Facebook: the link is clickable.
- WhatsApp: use the status or broadcast list, not a group without consent.
- Keep claims factual: no invented review counts, discounts or delivery guarantees.
